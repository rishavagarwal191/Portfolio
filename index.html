Hello! Let's break down Bias vs. Variance intuitively:

  • Bias (Underfitting): Your model is too simplistic (e.g., trying to fit a straight line to a complex curve). It misses key patterns in both training and testing data.
  • Variance (Overfitting): Your model is overly complex, memorizing noise in your training data. It performs brilliantly on train sets, but fails on unseen test data.

O(N) Efficiency Focus
Two Sum Optimal Solution 
def two_sum(nums: list[int], target: int) -> list[int]:
    """
    Find two indices such that their elements sum up to target.
    Time Complexity: O(N) single pass
    Space Complexity: O(N) auxiliary hash map
    """
    seen = {}
    for idx, num in enumerate(nums):
        complement = target - num
        if complement in seen:
            return [seen[complement], idx]
        seen[num] = idx
    return []
Why this works: Instead of the naive O(N²) nested loop check, a dictionary acts as an inverted lookup index, reducing the search of the complement to an instant O(1) average time per lookup.
